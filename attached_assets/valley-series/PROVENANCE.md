# The Valley series — artwork provenance

Original artwork for the Credyr website, generated on 2026-10-12 with Figma AI
`generate_image` (model `gemini-3.1-flash-image`) on the Credyr Figma team plan,
with the owner's approval. Text-only prompts; no reference images, no named
artists. Native output 1376×768 (16:9) and 1264×848 (3:2).

Files in this folder are the untouched originals. The web versions live in
`artifacts/credlyr/public/art/` (JPEG, quality ~90).

All four share this style block:

> Original soft gouache landscape painting, smooth matte brushwork with gentle
> paper texture, luminous warm light, harmonious palette of apricot, peach and
> soft rose, warm cream highlights, rich sage and olive greens, pale blue water
> reflecting the sky, touches of golden ochre. Calm, serene, gallery-quality
> editorial illustration. … No people, no animals, no buildings, no text, no
> frame, no border, edge-to-edge artwork.

Composition prompts (abridged):

- **Hero** — high vantage point over a wide valley; horizon ~25% from the top
  under glowing clouds; a winding river emerges from the horizon and meanders
  in S-curves through the middle toward the lower left; bottom 30% calm pale
  meadow. (Second attempt; the first placed the river too low for the hero fade.)
- **Sky** — wide open sky above the valley; peach and rose clouds framing the
  left and right edges; calm open centre for text; low strip of hills and river
  along the bottom 15%.
- **Dusk** — the valley at dusk from a high vantage point; amber horizon fading
  to dusky rose and violet-blue; calm open upper centre for text; river
  reflecting the sky; bottom 35% deep, near-uniform dark meadow fading to
  near-black.
- **Riverbank** — close view of a quiet riverbank; grasses and cream and pale
  yellow wildflowers in the foreground; calm river mirroring the sky; soft
  trees and hills beyond.
